# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in PewwTerm to be a respectful, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or appearance.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and critique ideas, code and behaviour rather than people.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy: do not share another person's files, paths, logs or private
  messages without permission.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption or deliberate bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, review
discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in
a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's
GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report. Maintainers will
aim to respond promptly and will keep reports confidential to the extent
possible.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is project-specific and is not a substitute for GitHub's
community guidelines or applicable law.
